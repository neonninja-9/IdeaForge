## 2026-08-01 - Form Accessibility and Async Buttons
**Learning:** Found that some form elements in this app (like `CommentForm`) rely solely on placeholder text for visual context without providing an explicit or visually-hidden `<label>` for screen readers. Also observed an opportunity to standardize async button states with a loading spinner and disabled styling, consistent with other components like `SubmitIdeaForm`.
**Action:** Always ensure that textareas and inputs have a proper `<label>` (using `sr-only` if a visual label is omitted for design reasons) and standardizing the async submit buttons by adding an animated spinner and `disabled:cursor-not-allowed` styles.
