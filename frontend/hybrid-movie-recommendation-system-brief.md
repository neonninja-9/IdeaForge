## Problem
Users often struggle to discover new movies due to information overload and generic recommendations. Traditional single-model recommendation engines frequently suffer from cold-start issues or lack diversity in their suggestions.

## Solution
Build a hybrid recommendation engine that combines collaborative filtering and content-based filtering techniques. This approach leverages user rating history alongside movie metadata to deliver highly personalized and diverse recommendations.

## Impact
Enhances user engagement and satisfaction by significantly reducing search friction and offering tailored content discovery.

## Suggested Tech Stack
A simple start: React + TypeScript, Firebase
