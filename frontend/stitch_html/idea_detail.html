Gasless NFT Minting API

block_smith
Author

The Problem

High gas fees on Ethereum and other L2s create a massive barrier for non-crypto native users to participate in NFT ecosystems. Users are forced to acquire native tokens simply to interact with a smart contract, leading to a fragmented and high-friction onboarding experience.

Proposed Solution

A backend-as-a-service API that abstracts away wallet connections and gas fees for end users. It uses meta-transactions (EIP-2771) to allow users to claim digital assets using just an email or social login, while the developer sponsors the gas via a funding dashboard.

This allows brands and creators to distribute digital collectibles without forcing their audience to understand seed phrases or gas pricing mechanics.
